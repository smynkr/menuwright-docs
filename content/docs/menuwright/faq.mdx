## Do I need a POS system to use MenuWright?

No. The fastest path is connecting Square, which syncs your menu and sales automatically. Without a POS, add menu items in the dashboard and import sales history from CSV or Excel — or explore instantly with realistic sample data. See [Square connection](/menuwright/square) and [CSV import](/menuwright/csv-import).

## Which POS systems are supported?

**Square** today, in sandbox mode while the production app is under review. The POS adapter interface is designed so **Toast** and **Clover** can be added without touching the analysis or UI — but they are not implemented yet.

## What if I don't have food cost data?

MenuWright still works — it classifies by popularity and revenue. Once items have food costs, it unlocks true contribution margins and the full profit-based matrix. See [the matrix methodology](/menuwright/menu-matrix).

## How are the AI recommendations generated?

MenuWright analyzes your menu matrix and sales data and produces specific, prioritized recommendations — reprice, promote, bundle, or cut — with a projected monthly dollar impact where one can be estimated. The AI layer uses an OpenAI-compatible interface to a primary and fallback provider; generation is a candidate step, and you decide what to act on. Recommendations fail gracefully: if the provider is unreachable, the analysis completes without recommendations rather than blocking.

## Is my restaurant's data secure?

Every record is scoped to your restaurant, and Square credentials are encrypted at rest. Tokens are deleted if you disconnect. The connection is read-only — MenuWright never modifies your Square data. See the [privacy policy](https://menuwright.com/privacy).

## What does each plan include?

Free: 1 analysis and 3 AI recommendation sets per month. Pro ($99/month): 4 analyses and 20 recommendation sets, plus reports, with metered overage beyond. See [billing and usage](/menuwright/billing) for the full contract.

## Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Pro is a month-to-month subscription with no long-term commitment — manage your plan from your billing settings.

## Why can't I connect my real Square account?

Production Square onboarding waits for Square's app review of MenuWright's production app. The integration is implemented and tested against the sandbox environment today; this documentation will be updated when production approval lands.

## What reports will I get?

Pro restaurants receive a weekly email digest (Monday, 8 AM local) and a monthly PDF deep dive (1st of the month, 8 AM local), both scheduled per-restaurant timezone. See [reports](/menuwright/reports).

## What is the menu engineering matrix?

It's based on the Kasavana–Smith menu engineering method (1982): every item is classified by profitability (contribution margin) and popularity (menu mix), landing in one of four quadrants — Stars, Crowd Pleasers, Hidden Gems, and Leftovers. See [the methodology](/menuwright/menu-matrix).

## What happens to my data if I leave?

Disconnecting Square deletes the stored credentials immediately. Data is scoped to your restaurant account; reach out to [support@menuwright.com](mailto:support@menuwright.com) for account deletion.

## Does MenuWright write to my Square account?

Never. The integration is strictly read-only: catalog and sales aggregates only. MenuWright never stores card or customer payment details.

## What's on the roadmap?

Toast and Clover adapters, consultant multi-restaurant mode, and an impact tracker are the headline directions — not yet available.
