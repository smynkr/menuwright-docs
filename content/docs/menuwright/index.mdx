## What MenuWright is for

Independent restaurants run on tight margins, and most menu decisions are guesses. MenuWright uses available sales and cost data to suggest which items to protect, promote, reprice, or cut, with a dollar impact estimate when one can be supported.

It is built for owners and operators who want a prioritized action, with an estimate where available, rather than another dashboard to interpret.

## FAQ

<Accordions>
  <Accordion title="Do I need a POS system?">
    Square's automated catalog and sales sync is currently described as a sandbox integration; production onboarding remains gated on app review. Without a POS, use CSV/Excel imports or sample data.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What if I don't have food costs?">
    MenuWright supports cost-based classifications for items with costs. Items missing costs can receive provisional revenue-share classifications; with no food costs, revenue share is only a profitability proxy. Add verified costs for every item to classify every item's profitability using contribution margins.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Where does my data live?">
    Every record is scoped to your restaurant, and Square credentials are encrypted at rest. See the [FAQ](/menuwright/faq) for the data boundaries.
  </Accordion>
</Accordions>
