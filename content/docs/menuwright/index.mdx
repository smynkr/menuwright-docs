## What MenuWright is for

Independent restaurants run on tight margins, and most menu decisions are guesses. MenuWright turns the sales and cost data a restaurant already has into a clear answer: which items to protect, promote, reprice, or cut — and roughly how many dollars each move is worth.

It is built for the owner or operator who wants an opinionated next action, not another dashboard to interpret. Every recommendation names the item, the action, and the projected impact.

## FAQ

<Accordions>
  <Accordion title="Do I need a POS system?">
    No. Connect Square for automatic catalog and sales sync, or import your menu and sales history as CSV/Excel. A sample-data mode lets you explore the workflow without any data.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What if I don't have food costs?">
    MenuWright still works — it classifies by popularity and revenue. Once items have food costs, it unlocks true contribution margins and the full profit-based matrix.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Where does my data live?">
    Every record is scoped to your restaurant, and Square credentials are encrypted at rest. See the [FAQ](/menuwright/faq) for the data boundaries.
  </Accordion>
</Accordions>
