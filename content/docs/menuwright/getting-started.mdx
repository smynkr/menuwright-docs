MenuWright compares item popularity with a profitability signal: contribution margin for items with known food costs and a revenue-share proxy for items without them. Its AI can suggest actions; a projected monthly dollar impact appears only when the product can estimate one. This guide walks through sign-up, data import, and your first analysis.

## Prerequisites

- An email address to create your account
- One of: a **Square account** to sync automatically, a **CSV or Excel file** of sales history, or no data at all (sample-data mode)

## 1. Create your account

Sign up at [menuwright.com](https://menuwright.com). You'll verify your email, then land in the onboarding wizard.

<Callout type="warn">
  MenuWright is in active development. Square connections run against the sandbox environment today — real-restaurant Square onboarding waits for production app review. CSV import and sample data work fully now.
</Callout>

## 2. Choose how to bring your data

The onboarding wizard offers three paths:

| Path                 | What it does                                                                                                                                                                |
| -------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Connect Square**   | OAuth flow that syncs your catalog and \~90 days of sales automatically, then runs a first analysis. See the [Square guide](/menuwright/square).                            |
| **Import CSV/Excel** | Upload sales history with columns for item name, date, quantity sold, and gross revenue; optionally include food costs. See the [CSV import guide](/menuwright/csv-import). |
| **Sample data**      | Load a realistic demo restaurant instantly — useful for exploring the workflow before connecting real data.                                                                 |

You can switch paths later from the dashboard's Data section.

## 3. Review your menu items

After data loads, review the imported menu items and fix any category mismatches. If you imported sales for items that don't exist yet, MenuWright can create them for you (see the CSV guide's `create_missing_items` behavior).

## 4. Enter food costs

Enter food costs for as many items as possible. Items with usable costs use contribution-margin classification. With partial coverage, uncoded items receive a provisional revenue-share classification; with no usable costs, revenue share is a profitability proxy for all items. Every active item needs a usable cost for a complete contribution-margin view. See the [menu matrix guide](/menuwright/menu-matrix) for details.

## 5. Run your first analysis

Choose an analysis period and run. MenuWright:

1. Computes menu mix (popularity) and contribution margins only for items with usable food costs; items without costs use a revenue-share profitability signal.
2. Places each item in one of four quadrants using its applicable signal — contribution margin where costs are known, revenue share where they are not.
3. Generates AI recommendations; a projected monthly dollar impact appears only when one can be estimated.
4. Saves an immutable snapshot so you can track changes over time

Without complete usable food-cost coverage, the profitability signal is not a true contribution margin for every item; no-cost analysis cannot show which items earn or lose money. Recommendations are suggestions, and any projected impact is an estimate rather than a guarantee.

## 6. Act on the recommendations

The dashboard's Recommendations feed lists prioritized actions and displays projected impact only when one can be estimated. Accept or dismiss each one — your decisions feed back into future recommendation quality. When you accept a recommendation, MenuWright tracks the item's revenue before and after the action; it may show an observed revenue change once both windows have at least 7 days of recorded sales. See the [FAQ](/menuwright/faq) for what that number does and doesn't mean.

## What you'll see

| Surface             | What it shows                                                                                                                                               |
| ------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Menu matrix**     | Interactive scatter plot of popularity × profitability with quadrant shading                                                                                |
| **Recommendations** | Prioritized AI suggestions, with impact estimates when available and an observed revenue change for accepted actions when measurement windows are complete. |
| **Insights**        | Bundle opportunities, cannibalization detection, and demand forecasting                                                                                     |
| **Trends**          | Revenue, contribution-margin trends when cost inputs support them, and classification movement over time                                                    |
| **Data**            | Menu items, food costs, POS sync history, and manual sync; CSV/Excel upload results are shown separately, not as POS sync history                           |
| **Settings**        | Restaurant profile, Square connection, and billing                                                                                                          |
