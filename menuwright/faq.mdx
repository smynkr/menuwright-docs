## Do I need a POS system to use MenuWright?

No. The fastest path is connecting Square, which syncs your menu and sales automatically. Without a POS, add menu items in the dashboard and import sales history from CSV or Excel — or explore instantly with realistic sample data. See [Square connection](/menuwright/square) and [CSV import](/menuwright/csv-import).

## Which POS systems are supported?

**Square** today, in sandbox mode while the production app is under review. The POS adapter interface is designed so **Toast** and **Clover** can be added without touching the analysis or UI — but they are not implemented yet.

## What if I don't have food cost data?

MenuWright uses revenue share as a profitability proxy when no food costs are available; that is not a contribution margin. With partial coverage, items with usable costs use contribution margin and uncoded items remain provisional. A complete contribution-margin view requires a usable food cost for every active item. See [the matrix methodology](/menuwright/menu-matrix).

## How are the AI recommendations generated?

MenuWright analyzes your menu matrix and sales data and produces specific, prioritized recommendations — reprice, promote, bundle, or cut — with a projected monthly dollar impact where one can be estimated. The AI layer uses an OpenAI-compatible interface to a primary and fallback provider; generation is a candidate step, and you decide what to act on. Recommendations fail gracefully: if the provider is unreachable, the analysis completes without recommendations rather than blocking.

## What is the "observed revenue change" on accepted recommendations?

When you accept a recommendation, MenuWright anchors a closed-loop measurement: it compares the item's average daily revenue in matched calendar windows before and after the action day (the action day itself is excluded). Once both windows have at least 7 days with recorded sales, the recommendation card shows an **observed revenue change** alongside the original projection — for example, "+$380/mo observed" next to "+$410/mo projected."

The windows use a growing matched calendar span: `span = min(30, days_since_action)`. The comparison stays weekday-mixed unless the span is a whole week, so matching lengths keeps it symmetric but does not by itself remove weekday-mix bias. Averages divide by the calendar span, not by the number of days with records, so sparse sellers aren't inflated.

<Note>
  This is an observed before/after revenue change, <strong>not</strong> an attributable impact. An uncontrolled comparison on a single item cannot separate the recommendation's effect from a concurrent price change, promotion, or seasonal shift. The label says "observed revenue change" deliberately.
</Note>

The number only appears when the data honestly supports it — if either window has fewer than 7 days with recorded sales, no figure is shown rather than a fabricated one. Negative deltas are included honestly.

## Is my restaurant's data secure?

Every record is scoped to your restaurant, and Square credentials are encrypted at rest. Tokens are revoked and deleted when you disconnect. The connection is read-only — MenuWright never modifies your Square data. See the [privacy policy](https://menuwright.com/privacy).

## What does each plan include?

Free: 1 analysis per month plus a preview of up to one AI recommendation when available. Core ($29/month): unlimited analyses and the complete AI recommendation set, with dollar-impact estimates where available. Pro ($99/month): everything in Core plus priority support and early access to new features. All paid plans are flat-rate — no usage meters, no overage. See [billing and plans](/menuwright/billing) for the full contract.

## Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Core and Pro are month-to-month subscriptions with no long-term commitment — manage your plan from your billing settings.

## Why can't I connect my real Square account?

Production Square onboarding waits for Square's app review of MenuWright's production app. The integration is implemented and tested against the sandbox environment today; this documentation will be updated when production approval lands.

## What reports will I get?

The backend defines a weekly HTML digest and a monthly PDF task. Their dispatchers check hourly during a restaurant's local 8 AM hour; the monthly task also requires the first day of the month. A send attempt needs a completed analysis and an active owner email, but this source configuration does not prove that a scheduler or worker is active or that a message will be delivered. Provider acceptance is not proof that a message reached an inbox. When sent, Free may receive up to one available recommendation preview (or none); paid monthly PDFs can include the full available set, while weekly digests display at most three recommendations. See [reports](/menuwright/reports).

## What is the menu engineering matrix?

It's based on the Kasavana–Smith menu engineering method (1982): each item is classified by popularity (menu mix) and an applicable profitability signal — contribution margin when a usable food cost is known, or a revenue-share proxy without one. With partial cost coverage, uncoded items are provisional; with no costs, revenue share is the proxy for all items. See [the methodology](/menuwright/menu-matrix) for the cost-coverage rules and four quadrants.

## What happens to my data if I leave?

Disconnecting Square revokes the OAuth grant and deletes the stored credentials immediately. Data is scoped to your restaurant account; reach out to [support@menuwright.com](mailto:support@menuwright.com) for account deletion.

## Does MenuWright write to my Square account?

Never. The integration is strictly read-only: catalog and sales aggregates only. MenuWright never stores card or customer payment details.
