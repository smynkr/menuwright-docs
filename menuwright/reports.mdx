MenuWright turns its analysis into two recurring reports, scheduled around each restaurant's own timezone.

<Callout>
  Reports are a **Pro** feature. Free accounts see an upgrade call to action instead.
</Callout>

## Weekly email digest

Every Monday at **8 AM local time**, Pro restaurants receive a plain-HTML email digest with:

- The top recommendations and their projected impact
- Key metric changes from the previous week
- Quick-action links into the dashboard

## Monthly PDF deep dive

On the first of the month at **8 AM local time**, Pro restaurants receive a full PDF report:

- The complete menu matrix visualization
- An item-by-item analysis table
- Month-over-month trend charts
- All AI recommendations with rationale
- An executive summary — the "you're leaving $X/mo on the table" number

## Timezone-aware scheduling

Each restaurant declares a timezone. The dispatch tasks check the **local hour** per restaurant — so a West Coast restaurant gets its digest at 8 AM PT, not 8 AM UTC. Dispatchers run hourly and deduplicate:

- Weekly digests are skipped if one was already sent in the last 5 days
- Monthly reports are skipped if one was already sent in the last 27 days

## Delivery

| Detail | Value |
|---|---|
| **Weekly digest** | HTML email via Resend |
| **Monthly report** | PDF generated with WeasyPrint, attached to an email via Resend |
| **Sender** | `reports@menuwright.com` |

If the PDF renderer is unavailable, generation **fails loudly** rather than sending an HTML file disguised as a PDF.
